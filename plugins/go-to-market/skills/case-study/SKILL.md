# Case study

The most persuasive marketing is a customer like the buyer saying it worked, with numbers they stand behind. Make it easy for the customer to say yes, get every fact right, and let them approve every word.

## 1. Pick and ask

- Pick customers who got a clear result, look like the ICP, and are happy to be named. One strong story beats five vague ones.
- Ask permission first, and be specific: named or anonymous, logo, quote, numbers, and where it will appear. Offer something back, such as a link, visibility for their team, and a draft they control. Record the answer in the proof bank.

## 2. Interview, in 30 minutes

- **Before:** what was happening, what they tried, why they chose you, how they decided.
- **After:** what changed, how they measure it (the numbers and the period), what surprised them, what they'd tell a peer.
- **Next:** what's coming.

Ask permission to record, and note their exact words.

## 3. Verify

Every number comes from the customer's data or yours, with the period and how it was measured. Use the typical result, not the best day. Where the customer won't share a number, use a relative or qualitative statement they approve.

## 4. Write

- **Headline:** the result for this type of customer, for example "How a 40-person Shopify brand cut checkout time by 90%".
- **Snapshot:** the customer, the industry, the size, what they use, and the two or three headline results.
- **Challenge, approach, result,** in their words, with quotes.
- **What's next,** and a call to action for readers like them.
- **Short versions:** about 100 words for the site, one slide for sales, a quote bank, and a social post draft.

## 5. Approve

Send the full draft to the customer for approval; the person sends it. Nothing is published before the customer approves in writing. Record the approval and its date in the proof bank, and add the new proof points with their IDs.

## Gotchas

- A result the customer won't attribute to you doesn't belong in the story.
- An anonymous case study still needs approval when the customer could be recognised.
- Revisit after 12–18 months: are they still a customer, and is it still true?

## Output

`drafts/case-studies/<customer>.md` with every version, the approval status, and the new proof bank entries. The person publishes after the customer's approval.
