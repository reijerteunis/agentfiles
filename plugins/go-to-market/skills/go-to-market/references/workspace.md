# Go-to-market workspace

A private folder per product, for example `~/Code/life/gtm/<product>`. Strategy in markdown, one file per deal with its status in frontmatter, and `pipeline.md` generated by `scripts/deals.py`. When the product belongs to a company with its own CRM and drive, the company's systems hold the deals and contacts; keep only the method's files here.

```
<workspace>/
├── product.md                 the brief: product, customers, motion, 90-day goal, constraints, sales policy
├── strategy/
│   ├── icp.md                 profile, personas, triggers, disqualifiers (icp-definition)
│   ├── icp.json               the scoring rubric for score_accounts.py (icp-definition)
│   ├── positioning.md         (positioning)
│   ├── messaging.md           message house, persona matrix, objections (messaging)
│   ├── proof.md               the proof bank: every claim's evidence, date and permission (messaging)
│   ├── pricing.md             value metric, plans, price policy, discount rules (pricing-packaging)
│   └── channels.md            channel bets with targets and floors (channel-plan)
├── launches/YYYY-MM-<slug>.md launch plans and their reviews (launch-plan)
├── drafts/                    pages/, sequences/, proposals/, case-studies/ (nothing here is live)
├── accounts/<company>.md      account briefs for B2B prospects (outbound-sequence)
├── deals/<company>-<slug>.md  one per opportunity
├── exports/                   CSV exports for gtm-metrics
├── reviews/YYYY-Www.md        weekly and quarterly reviews
├── suppression.md             people who opted out or must never be contacted
└── pipeline.md                generated by deals.py; don't edit by hand
```

## product.md

```markdown
---
product: Checkout audit for Shopify stores
stage: pre-launch | first customers | repeatable | scaling
motion: founder-led | product-led | services | marketplace | b2c-subscription
goal_90d: "10 paying customers and €3k MRR by 2027-01-15"
quarter_target: 15000
currency: EUR
updated: 2026-10-13
---

## What it does, and for whom
## Current customers and evidence
## Constraints (discretion, regulated claims, countries, languages)
## Budget and hours per week
## Sales policy (list prices, the maximum discount without approval, payment terms, who approves exceptions)
```

`quarter_target` is the open pipeline or revenue target that `deals.py` measures coverage against, in `currency`.

## Deal file

```markdown
---
company: Brightcart
contact: Jo Jansen, Head of Ecommerce
source: warm intro
stage: discovery
value: 12000
close_date: 2026-12-15
next_step: "Send the proposal with two options"
next_step_due: 2026-10-20
icp_tier: A
updated: 2026-10-13
loss_reason:
---

## Context (pain, impact, critical event, decision process, people)
## Log (append only: date, what happened, what was agreed)
```

| Field | Values |
|---|---|
| `stage` | `lead` (named account and contact, no conversation yet) → `qualified` (fits the ICP, has a reason to buy now) → `discovery` (call done; pain, impact and decision process known) → `proposal` (sent) → `negotiation` (terms being agreed) → `won`, `lost` or `parked` |
| `source` | `warm intro`, `inbound`, `outbound`, `event`, `referral`, `partner`, `community` |
| `value` | Expected first-year value in the product's currency; required from `discovery` on |
| `loss_reason` | Required when lost: `price`, `timing`, `no decision`, `competitor`, `fit`, `other`; add the detail in the log |
| `probability` | Optional override (0–100) of the stage default: lead 5, qualified 10, discovery 25, proposal 50, negotiation 75. Say why in the log |
| `next_step_due` | Required for open deals; for parked deals it's the date to revisit |

`deals.py --check` rejects unknown stages and sources, malformed dates, open deals without a next step and due date, deals past `qualified` without a value, and lost deals without a reason.

## Account brief (accounts/<company>.md)

Five to ten sourced lines: what they do, their size, why they fit (tier and criteria), the trigger (why now), the contacts with business role and business email only, the source of each contact and the basis for contacting them, and the links with dates. No personal-life details, no data the outreach doesn't need.

## suppression.md

One line per person who opted out or must never be contacted: name, company, date. Check it before every draft. It's the only record kept after the rest of their data is deleted.

## Retention

Delete account briefs and contact details for prospects who didn't engage after 12 months, and immediately for anyone who asks. Closed deals keep the company, the dates, the value and the reason, not the personal notes.
