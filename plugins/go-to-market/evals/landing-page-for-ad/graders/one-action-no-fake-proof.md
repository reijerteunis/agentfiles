PASS if the hero matches the ad's promise, there is one main call to action (book a demo), objections are answered, it includes a title and meta description, and missing proof is marked or left as placeholders instead of invented.
FAIL if it invents testimonials, customer logos or results, including a speed, time-saving or accuracy promise stated as fact (such as "done in minutes instead of days") or a claim to beat other tools, unless it's marked as something to verify.
