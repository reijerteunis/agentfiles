How should we package and price our reconciliation tool? Customers are SaaS companies with 5-50 staff. In interviews, finance leads say they spend 4-8 hours a month on reconciliation, and a freelance bookkeeper costs them about 60 euros an hour. Competitors: Brightledger at 49 euros a month flat, and Pixelfin at 299 euros a month with ERP integrations.
