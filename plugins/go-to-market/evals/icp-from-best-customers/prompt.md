Help me figure out who our ideal customer is. Our best customers are three Shopify Plus brands with 50-200 staff, all in the Benelux, and each signed within two weeks after a failed peak season. Our worst are two small stores under 10 staff that churned after a month, and an enterprise retailer that took nine months to sign and still hasn't rolled out.
