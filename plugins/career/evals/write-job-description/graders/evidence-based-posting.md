PASS if the posting states the €95k–€115k range, describes what the hire will achieve (outcomes over the first months or year) rather than only listing duties, keeps the must-have requirements to five or fewer and states them as evidence of capability rather than years of experience, and is honest about at least one hard part of the role (such as on-call duty, legacy systems or incident pressure).
FAIL if any must-have is a number of years of experience, if it uses exclusionary wording (rockstar, ninja, young, digital native, or gendered terms), or if the pay range is missing.
