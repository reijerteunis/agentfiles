# Application answers

Forms are where good candidates lose to lazy answers. Answer every question as if the hiring manager reads it, which they sometimes do, and never let a form trick the person into a bad commitment.

## Before answering

Read `jd.md`, `fit.md`, `research.md`, `profile/master-cv.md`, `profile/stories.md` and `profile/preferences.md`. List every question with its type (free text, number, yes/no, select) and its character limit.

## Answer by type

- **Why us / why this role:** two specific reasons from the research and one result from the master CV that proves the fit. No generic praise.
- **"Tell us about a time…":** pick the best-matching story from `stories.md`. Compress it to situation, action and result within the limit. Use only the parts the person has actually told; if the story is still mostly TODO, ask them for it.
- **Salary expectations:** never anchor low and never disclose current or past pay. If a number is required, give a range starting at the `Target` in preferences. Use only the person's numbers: with just a minimum, write "from €88,000"; never invent the top of the range. If a range is allowed, say it's negotiable depending on total package and scope. In the Netherlands, say whether the figure includes the 8% holiday allowance (vakantiegeld), and a 13th month if there is one, so it's compared like for like.
- **Current or past salary:** don't disclose it, even when the form marks the question as required. Answer with the expectation instead ("from €88,000, depending on the total package"), and tell the person why: it anchors the offer to the old pay. If the field only accepts a number, don't put the target there as if it were the current pay, because that's untrue: tell the person, and let them choose between giving the real figure and asking the recruiter to discuss expectations instead. The EU Pay Transparency Directive (2023/970) bans employers from asking about pay history once national law applies it; the Netherlands plans 1 January 2027.
- **Notice period, start date, work authorisation, location:** exactly what preferences say. If preferences don't say, ask; never guess a legal or contractual fact.
- **Knock-out yes/no questions:** answer truthfully. If a truthful "no" disqualifies, tell the person before they start the form.
- **Optional demographic and diversity questions:** leave these to the person, and say so.
- **"Anything else?":** use it only for something that adds signal, such as the angle in one sentence or a referral name.

## Rules

- Facts only from the master CV and the stories, as everywhere in the career skills.
- **Never submit.** If you fill in a form in the browser, do it with the person watching, stop before the final submit, and tell them it's ready to review.
- Count characters for every limited field and stay at least 5% under the limit.

## Output

Write `answers.md`: each question, its limit, the answer, and the character count. Mark the answers that need the person's input with **INPUT NEEDED** at the top. In the reply, list what needs their input, if anything, and the file path.
