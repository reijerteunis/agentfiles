# Outreach message

The right message to the right person at the right moment beats ten applications into the void. Each one is short, specific to the reader, and easy to say yes to.

## Before writing

Read the application folder (`application.md`, `fit.md`, `research.md`) and the person's context skill, which holds the discretion rules and the voice. Find the recipient's name and role. A message to "Hiring Team" is a last resort.

## Message types

| Type | When | Length | Must contain |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hiring-manager note | Right after applying, or instead of applying cold | LinkedIn note ≤ 300 chars; email ≤ 120 words | The angle in one sentence, one hook from the research, and a small ask (a 20-minute call) |
| Recruiter reply | Within a day of their message | ≤ 120 words | Interest level, two must-know constraints (location, timing), and availability. No salary number unless they ask, then give the range from preferences |
| Referral ask | Before applying, to someone who works there | ≤ 120 words | Why this role, why them, a two-line blurb they can forward, and an easy out |
| Follow-up | 7–10 days after applying, again 7 days later | ≤ 80 words | The role and date applied, one new piece of value or a reminder of the angle, and the ask |
| Thank-you | Within 24 hours of each interview | ≤ 120 words | One specific moment from the conversation, one point reinforced, and enthusiasm for the next step |
| Withdrawal | When the person drops out | ≤ 60 words | Thanks, a clear decision, and the door left open |
| Reconnect | Warming up a past colleague | ≤ 100 words | Shared context, a genuine update, no ask in the first message |

Email subject lines are under 8 words and specific: "Data engineer application, Jane Doe".

## Rules

- **Drafts only.** With the Gmail connector, create a draft (never send). Otherwise write the draft into the application folder. The person sends it.
- **Discretion.** Use the person's personal channels, never work email or work accounts. Never contact the current employer's people, clients or partners about the search.
- **Truth.** Every fact comes from the master CV, the same as in the CV itself.
- No "just checking in", no "I hope this finds you well", no guilt-tripping in follow-ups.

## Output

The draft (subject and body, or the LinkedIn text with its character count), where it was saved, and the suggested send time. Log it in `application.md` and set the next follow-up date.
