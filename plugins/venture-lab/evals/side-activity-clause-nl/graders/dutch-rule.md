PASS if it explains that since 1 August 2022 article 7:653a BW makes a clause that bans or restricts side work void unless the employer has an objective reason (such as a conflict of interest, confidentiality or working-time limits), that this also applies to a contract signed in 2019, and that a refusal needs such a reason tied to this activity, which an unrelated consumer app makes hard to show; and it still advises keeping the work separate (own time and equipment, no employer data or code) and asking for HR's reason in writing or an employment lawyer if they persist.
FAIL if it treats the consent clause or HR's policy as binding by itself, says the 2022 rule covers only newer contracts, or advises simply accepting the refusal.
