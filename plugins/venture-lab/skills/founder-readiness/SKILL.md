# Founder readiness

Whether to start is partly about the idea and partly about the founder's life. Quitting too early burns runway; waiting forever lets the window close. Turn "someday" into a plan with triggers.

This is planning, not financial advice. For tax, pensions, mortgages or investments, recommend an accountant or financial advisor.

## Gather, privately and only what's needed

- **Money:** monthly fixed expenses, savings available for runway (excluding emergency funds), other household income, and the minimum monthly income that keeps life stable.
- **Time:** realistic hours per week now, which evenings and weekends, and family commitments.
- **Risk appetite:** what failure would cost them, financially and personally, and what they could live with.
- **Skills:** what the business needs (product, engineering, sales, domain, fundraising) against what the founder brings. The gaps decide whether a co-founder is needed.
- **The current job:** what leaving costs (salary, unvested equity, bonus timing, notice period, reputation) and what staying costs (time, energy, conflicts). Check the notice period and the contract with `side-project-check`.

Keep this in `profile/founder.md`, and keep it minimal. It's personal data.

## Compute the runway

Runway in months = available savings ÷ (monthly expenses − other household income). Show it plainly, and the runway with a 20% buffer for surprises. Under 12 months is tight for a venture-backed path; for a bootstrapped path, aim for first revenue well before the runway ends.

## Jump triggers

Agree on concrete, observable triggers that tell the founder when to move to the next stage, for example:

| Stage | Trigger to move on |
|---|---|
| Evenings and weekends → part-time or sabbatical | 3 signed LOIs or pilots, plus a clean side-project check |
| Part-time → full-time | €X in monthly recurring revenue, or pre-seed funding closed, or 12+ months of runway |
| Never jump on | enthusiasm alone, one big "maybe", or a deadline you set yourself without evidence |

## Output

`readiness.md` in the idea folder, or in `profile/` for the general case: the runway calculation, the skills-gap table, the co-founder need (yes, no, or which role), the opportunity cost, the jump triggers, and the staged plan with dates to revisit. Be candid when the honest answer is "not yet", and give the specific thing that would change it.
