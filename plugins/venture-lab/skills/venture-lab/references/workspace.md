# Venture lab workspace

A private folder, for example `~/Code/life/ventures`. One folder per idea; evidence in files; status in frontmatter; `pipeline.md` generated by `scripts/lab.py`.

```
<workspace>/
├── profile/
│   ├── founder.md          skills, unfair advantages, motivations, constraints, employment situation
│   └── criteria.md         what makes an idea worth it for this founder, and the default kill criteria
├── ideas/<slug>/
│   ├── idea.md             the card: frontmatter + problem, customer, why now, edge, model guess, log
│   ├── assumptions.md      the assumption map (experiment-design)
│   ├── interviews/YYYY-MM-DD-<who>.md
│   ├── experiments/NN-<slug>.md
│   ├── market.md           sizing, competitors, positioning (market-map)
│   ├── model.md            business model and unit economics (business-model)
│   └── decisions.md        gate decisions with their evidence (kill-or-continue)
└── pipeline.md             generated; don't edit by hand
```

## idea.md frontmatter

```yaml
---
title: Invoice extraction for small accountants
stage: capture | explore | validate | decide | prepare
status: active | parked | killed | launched
screen_score: 62          # from idea-screen, 0-100
riskiest_assumption: "Accountants will pay €50/month to skip manual entry"
next_step: "5 problem interviews with accountants"
decide_by: 2026-11-15     # the date of the next gate decision
created: 2026-10-14
---
```

`lab.py --check` rejects unknown stages and statuses, malformed dates, and active ideas without `next_step` or `decide_by`.

## Experiment file

```markdown
---
assumption: "Accountants will pay €50/month"
method: smoke test | fake door | concierge | pre-sale | prototype test | interviews
success: "≥ 10 sign-ups and ≥ 3 'pay now' clicks from 200 targeted visitors"
kill: "< 3 sign-ups"
start: 2026-10-20
end: 2026-11-03
result:
---

## Setup
## Result (filled in after the end date, before interpreting)
## Learning
```

Success and kill thresholds are written before the experiment starts and are never edited afterwards.
