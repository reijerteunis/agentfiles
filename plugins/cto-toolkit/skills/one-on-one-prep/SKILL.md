# 1:1 prep

A 1:1 is the report's meeting, not a status update. Prep makes it worth their time: remember what they said last time, notice what they did since, and bring the questions that open real conversations.

## Privacy rules

- Notes about a person are personal data (GDPR in the EU). Store them **only** in the private, company-approved location from the company context, never in a shared repo, a personal repo or a chat with anyone else.
- Write only what's needed and job-related, factual and neutral. No health, family or personal-life details unless the person raised them and a follow-up needs them.
- Never infer or record protected characteristics. Never carry one person's notes into another person's prep.
- The person may ask to see their notes. Write every line as if they will.

## Prep

1. Read the last 2–3 sets of notes and the open action items, on both sides.
2. Check the person's goals and their recent work: tickets and PRs from the tracker and code host. Read the titles and outcomes only; this is context, not surveillance.
3. Note anything that changed: projects, team, the org, workload.
4. Build the agenda:
   1. **Their topics first.** Ask them to add their own ahead of time.
   2. **Recognition:** one specific thing they did well since last time.
   3. **Blockers and support:** what's slowing them down, and what you can unblock.
   4. **Growth:** progress on their development goal (monthly at least).
   5. **Feedback both ways:** one thing for them, and ask what you could do better.
   6. **Actions:** review the open ones and agree new ones.
5. Prepare 3 questions for the moment, for example "What's the most frustrating part of your week?", "What would make the next quarter a great one for you?", "What's one thing you'd change about how we work?"

## After the meeting

Capture the notes in `people/<person>/1on1-YYYY-MM-DD.md` in the private location: the topics discussed, decisions, actions with owners, and follow-ups for next time. Keep it to five to ten lines. Facts and agreements, not judgments.
