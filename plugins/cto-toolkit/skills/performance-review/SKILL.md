# Performance review

A good review holds no surprises, is backed by examples, and leaves the person knowing exactly what great looks like next. You help gather evidence and write clearly. **The manager makes and owns every judgment and rating.**

## Ground rules

- **Human judgment decides.** In the EU, AI used to evaluate workers' performance is a high-risk use under the AI Act (Annex III, employment). Keep the manager's assessment in charge, keep the evidence trail, and don't present AI output as the evaluation.
- **Privacy.** This is personal data. Work only in the private, company-approved location. Use only job-related evidence, and never infer or record protected characteristics.
- **No surprises.** Nothing in the review should be news to the person. If it would be, flag it to the manager: it should have come up in a 1:1 first.

## Steps

1. **Evidence.** For the review period, collect:
   - goals and how they turned out
   - delivered work with outcomes (from the tracker and code host, by title and result)
   - peer and stakeholder feedback
   - 1:1 notes
   - the person's self-review

   Weigh the whole period, not just the last month.
2. **Expectations.** Use the career ladder or level descriptions if the company has them. Assess impact (what they achieved) and behaviours (how), against their level.
3. **Draft:**
   - **Summary**, three lines.
   - **Impact:** 3–5 outcomes with examples.
   - **Strengths**, specific.
   - **Growth areas:** 1–3, each with an example, why it matters at the next level, and support offered.
   - **Goals for the next cycle.**
4. **Bias check:**
   - **recency** (does the evidence cover the full period?)
   - **halo or horn** (one strong area colouring the rest)
   - **leniency or strictness** compared with peers
   - **similarity bias**
   - **coded language:** personality words such as "abrasive" or "emotional" for some people and "assertive" for others; describe behaviour and its impact instead
   - **unfounded ratings:** any rating without evidence
5. **Calibration notes:** the proposed rating, the strongest evidence for it, and the main counter-evidence. Keep them for the calibration session.

## Output

`people/<person>/review-<cycle>.md` in the private location: the draft review, the evidence list with sources, the bias-check notes and the calibration notes. Mark it **DRAFT: manager to edit and own**.
