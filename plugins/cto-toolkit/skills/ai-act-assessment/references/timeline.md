# AI Act timeline

**As of October 2026.** The AI Act is Regulation (EU) 2024/1689. The Digital Omnibus on AI amended several dates; it entered into force on 27 July 2026. Commentary sources differ on some details, so verify against the consolidated text on [EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj) before relying on a date for a real decision, and update this file when you do.

| Applies from | What | Note |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Aug 2024 | The Act enters into force | |
| 2 Feb 2025 | Prohibited practices (Article 5) and AI literacy (Article 4) | |
| 2 Aug 2025 | General-purpose AI model obligations, governance and penalties | |
| 2 Aug 2026 | Transparency duties (Article 50): AI interaction disclosure, deepfake labelling | not deferred by the Omnibus |
| 2 Dec 2026 | Machine-readable marking of synthetic content, for generative systems already on the market before 2 Aug 2026 | grace period added by the Omnibus; newer systems: 2 Aug 2026 |
| 2 Dec 2026 | Additional prohibited practices added by the Omnibus | check the amended Article 5 for the exact list |
| 2 Dec 2027 | High-risk obligations for **Annex III** systems (employment, education, credit, biometrics, critical infrastructure and others) | was 2 Aug 2026 before the Omnibus |
| 2 Aug 2028 | High-risk obligations for **Annex I** systems (AI in products under EU product legislation) | was 2 Aug 2027 before the Omnibus |

Sources consulted for the Omnibus changes, all secondary: [Gibson Dunn](https://www.gibsondunn.com/eu-ai-act-omnibus-agreement-postponed-high-risk-deadlines-and-other-key-changes/), [Cooley](https://cdp.cooley.com/digital-ai-omnibus-delays-key-deadlines-introduces-new-rules/), [Cloud Security Alliance](https://labs.cloudsecurityalliance.org/research/csa-research-note-eu-ai-act-omnibus-vii-deadline-delay-20260/).
