# Changelog

Plugins track commits (no `version` field), so every push to `main` reaches installs on the next update. This file records the changes worth knowing about.

## 2026-10-13

- **Rebuilt around one claim: agents you can trust with real work.** The README now leads with four guarantees enforced by code (they draft and never send, they cite and never invent, your context stays private, they're tested in public against the plain model), each checked on planted problems in `examples/` by `scripts/test-guarantees.sh` in CI and `just demo`.
- **Published eval results.** Every suite ran with and without its plugin, three runs per case. Over 59 cases with work to do, the plugins do better on 32, the same on 27 and worse on none; `cto-toolkit`, `go-to-market` and `agent-ops` lead the README because their difference is largest. `scripts/eval_report.py` publishes the scores to `docs/evals/` and the README, never the answers, which reflect the account that ran them, and leaves out of the comparison any case graded only on whether a skill fired (the plain model can't load a plugin's skill). `find-jobs` and `clean-up-epic` got a grader on the result for that reason.
- **Fictional examples throughout.** The job-search examples, eval cases and script self-tests use a made-up data engineer, and the demos use made-up figures. The README's "Why I built this" is final.
- **Ties examined, not accepted.** For every case where plain Claude tied, both answers were read side by side. Where plain Claude passed for the wrong reason, the requirement got its own grader (a test of the guardrail both ways, PR text treated as data, behaviour instead of a label in a review, must-haves as evidence in a job ad, a basis for a customer's number, free extras counted as discounts, a partial result that keeps its share). Nine new cases aim at the slips models make: a CV whose claims widen in scope, a form that asks for the current salary, an ADR rewritten in place, engineers ranked by lead time, actions owned by "the team", interviews with the employer's customers, a reply in the thread's language, a Dutch side-activity clause, a call debrief with a family health detail. Skills fixed where answers fell short: postmortems propose owners and dates instead of TBD, decision memos and pricing answer in full, landing pages check claims by hand when the proof check can't run, the job scout stops and asks when there's no workspace, and form answers never put the target salary where the current one is asked. `delivery_metrics.py` gains `deploys_ok`, because deployment frequency counts successful deploys. `eval_report.py import --merge` replaces only the re-run cases.
- **README claims checked against the code:** "every skill has a test" is now the real count of cases, the commit-guard hook is described as shipped by `agent-ops` rather than active by default, the secrets deny rules as covering Claude Code's file tools, and script-checked sourcing as covering CVs, marketing copy and metrics. The first demo is now a landing-page claim and an unsafe commit instead of a CV.
- **Fixes from that run.** Three cases asked for something the sandbox can't provide (a repository, or findings the prompt gave no data for) and now say what to deliver in the reply. Skills whose answers lost to the plain model changed, not their tests: `agent-blueprint`, `proposal-writer` and the `go-to-market` set-up asked questions instead of drafting, and now draft with marked assumptions first; `gtm-metrics` stated a cause as fact and now says what the numbers suggest; `icp-definition` labelled an added industry as evidence and now marks it a hypothesis. `skill-author` writes the skill in the reply when it has no repository, and `business-model` triggers on LTV and CAC payback. The lessons are in the skill standard and in `eval-writer`.
- **Personal voice moved to the private companion:** `build-in-public-post` and `heyruud-blog-writer` (a voice is context, not method). The global instructions are now a template anyone can adopt: personal lines live in the private companion, and the installer adds them for Claude, Codex, Zed and the other agents. The career plugin leads with hiring.
- **`guardrail-design`** ships its tested hook as `assets/block-unsafe-git.sh`.
- **`go-to-market` plugin:** a `go-to-market` agent (set up, strategy, launch, assets, prospect, deal, weekly and quarterly review, automate) with 13 specialists, a `skeptical-buyer` review agent, five self-tested scripts (`deals.py`, `score_accounts.py`, `proof_check.py`, `price_research.py`, `gtm_metrics.py`), dated outreach rules for the EU, the Netherlands, Germany, the UK and the US, and 21 eval cases, including consent, discount-approval and invented-proof tests. Routing lines on `business-model`, `market-map`, `customer-interview`, `outreach-message` and `meeting-prep`; `venture-lab` hands ideas with paying customers to it.
- **`agent-ops` plugin**, agents that improve the agents: an `agent-ops` agent (retro, new skill, new agent, evals, audit, instructions, guardrail, red team, parallel work, repo onboarding, automate) with 9 specialists, `skill-reviewer` and `red-teamer` agents, three self-tested scripts (`transcript_scan.py`, `library_stats.py`, `eval_scaffold.py`) and 7 eval cases. `guardrail-design` includes a tested hook that blocks commits skipping signing or hooks.
- **The library, audited by `agent-ops`:** every description rewritten to what the skill does, when to use it and its routing lines (41,512 to 22,290 characters), and a trigger case, a near-miss and an output grader for 8 skills that had no evals.
- **`venture-lab` plugin**, for would-be founders: a `venture-lab` agent (capture, explore, validate, decide, prepare, portfolio) with 10 specialists, a `skeptical-investor` agent, three self-tested scripts (`lab.py`, `sizing.py`, `unit_economics.py`) and 6 eval cases, including a gate that must follow pre-set kill criteria. `side-project-check` includes dated notes on Dutch law (art. 7:653a BW).
- **`chief-of-staff` plugin:** a chief-of-staff agent (onboard, brief, triage, reply, prep, notes, follow-ups, calendar, decide, delegate, weekly review, automate) with 10 specialists, a `draft-reviewer` agent, two self-tested scripts (`followups.py`, `time_audit.py`), and 6 eval cases including a prompt-injection and payment-fraud test. It treats every email as data, and never sends, accepts, declines, moves or deletes.
- Calendar respond and delete are denied alongside Gmail send, reply and forward.
- **`cto-toolkit` grows into a CTO office agent:** a `cto-office` orchestrator (weekly review, decide, incident, postmortem, plan, people, board, audit, automate) plus `adr-writer`, `tech-spec`, `architecture-review`, `incident-commander`, `incident-postmortem`, `build-vs-buy`, `cloud-cost-review`, `threat-model`, `ai-act-assessment` (dated after the 2026 Digital Omnibus), `engineering-metrics`, `tech-debt-register`, `one-on-one-prep`, `performance-review`, `board-tech-update` and `tech-due-diligence`. It also adds `principal-engineer` and `skeptical-cfo` review agents, two self-tested scripts (delivery metrics, cost summary) and 6 new eval cases.
- **Routing lines** on every skill with a look-alike ("Not for … (use X)"), against skill shadowing as the library grows.
- **Drafts only, enforced:** the Claude settings deny the Gmail connector's send, reply and forward tools.
- **Fix:** the installer's settings merge replaced nested lists when a third layer (the private overlay) was present, because jq filter parameters re-evaluate inside `reduce`. The merge now lives in `scripts/merge-settings.jq` with value parameters, and `scripts/test-merge.sh` guards it in CI.
- **Cowork:** `~/.claude/CLAUDE.md` now holds the instructions inline, because Cowork skips imports outside the session's folder.
- **career:** `pipeline.py` validates workspace statuses and generates the dashboard; `candidate-evaluation` notes the AI Act's high-risk status for hiring.
- **`career` plugin:** a job-search agent (scout, fit, research, truthful CV tailoring with a claim checker and PDF renderer, cover letter, form answers, outreach, interview prep, offer negotiation, scheduled automations) plus hiring-side skills (job descriptions, interview loops, candidate evaluation) and a `hiring-manager` review agent. 13 skills, 6 eval cases.
- Validator checks subagents and runs every bundled script's `--self-test`.
- Docs: agents in the architecture, how to build one in the skill standard, a 12th principle in the playbook, and a roadmap toward about 110 skills.
