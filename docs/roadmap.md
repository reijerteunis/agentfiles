# Roadmap

Where this library is going: about 125 skills in nine domains. Plugins are the on/off switch: enable the packs you need right now, because skill selection gets less accurate as the enabled catalog grows past about 30 skills. Each domain grows into an **agent**, not a pile of skills (see [architecture](architecture.md#agents)). Domains ship one at a time, complete and tested: orchestrator, specialists, context, state, gates and evals. Every shipped domain is tested with and without its plugin over three runs, and the README leads with the ones whose tests show the largest difference.

## How a domain ships

1. The **agent** (orchestrator skill) and its workflow, with human gates.
2. The **specialists** it calls, each usable on its own.
3. The **context** it needs from the private repo, and the **state** layout in the private workspace.
4. **Evals:** a trigger case and a near-miss per skill, plus an end-to-end case for the agent.
5. **Automations:** the scheduled runs, if the domain has a rhythm.
6. **Evidence:** the eval scores published with `just eval-report`, including the cases where the plain model does just as well.
